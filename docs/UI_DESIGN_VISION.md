# UI design vision: Integrated War Room

Status: **APPROVED M7 DESIGN NORTH STAR**. M7.1 is **IMPLEMENTED — VISUAL DIRECTION REVIEW PENDING**; see [M7.1 visual direction review](M7_1_VISUAL_DIRECTION_REVIEW.md). Three prototypes are available, but no direction is selected. This vision remains a north star, not a frozen component specification.

## Direction

The selected concept is **Integrated War Room — Hybrid Tactical Surface**. Its
primary visual reference is the original Integrated War Room mockup selected
during product planning. That mockup establishes visual and interaction
direction; it does not establish fictional numerical requirements, fixed screen
geometry, mandatory charts, or a premature component specification.

The application should open with the **Living League** as an immersive view of
the current competitive situation. A contextual **Strategic Canvas** supports
transaction and scenario exploration. The **Digital Football Strategy Table**
provides the football-specific visual language. The atmosphere combines Mission
Control clarity, War Room competitive identity, and Strategy Lab interaction.
Discovery and Research are complementary depths of the same application rather
than separate products.

M7 will develop this experience progressively and iteratively against accepted,
versioned backend contracts. Prototype presentation must never become an
independent source of league rules, transaction legality, statistics, or
fictional analytical results.

## Principles

- Be immersive rather than dashboard-first.
- Feel like premium professional software, not a video game.
- Use football-specific visual language instead of generic science-fiction
  styling.
- Prefer tactile, contextual, and spatial interactions where they improve
  understanding.
- Do not require 3D navigation or artificial game mechanics.
- Avoid gratuitous animation, excessive neon, and decorative analytics.
- Keep familiar direct controls available.
- Retain accessible detailed results and tables wherever they communicate more
  effectively.
- Make every numerical visualization reflect the actual analytical meaning,
  provenance, completeness, and uncertainty of its source result.
- Put usability, responsiveness, accessibility, and clarity ahead of spectacle.

This vision guides later design decisions while leaving M7 free to validate
specific layouts, components, interactions, accessibility behavior, and
performance with representative workflows.
