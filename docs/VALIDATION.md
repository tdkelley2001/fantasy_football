## Assurance scope and evidence status

Assurance answers five different questions. Correctly executing a provisional model establishes neither calibrated forecasts nor useful decisions nor a release-ready application. Current [capabilities](../README.md#current-capabilities) and the following evidence map describe bounded support, not certification.

| Question | Evidence that can establish a bounded claim | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Implementation correctness | Independent finite-universe references, invariants, adversarial inputs and cross-layer consistency (V1) | Rules not independently sourced, every finite-float extreme, or optimality outside the covered universe |
| Numerical/statistical correctness | Reconciled path counts, frozen draws/goldens, estimator/interval references, worker/recovery parity (V2) | Exact sequential coverage merely from reproducibility, or empirical football calibration |
| Empirical validity | Authentic temporally isolated forecast/actual evidence and support/calibration diagnostics (V3) | Promotion from retrospective pairs, synthetic fixtures or low Monte Carlo error |
| Decision validity | Covered candidate universe, paired championship comparisons, proxy/full-path validation and robust selection (V4) | Offer acceptance, waiver success or a global optimum after screening/caps |
| Operational readiness | Representative repeated workload, storage/security/lifecycle evidence and independent release disposition (V5) | Clean-machine/browser/process/live behavior inferred from injected worker fixtures |

Use [D2's evidence vocabulary](DATA.md#evidence-vocabulary) exactly: **Verified live** is a bounded authorized observation; **Verified by tests** is synthetic/sanitized code evidence; **Unverified** needs evidence; **Unsupported** is unavailable/unsafe in the investigated source or consumer. A rule interpretation, source label, schema/hash, approved assumption or passed test is not interchangeable with live semantic verification. No live verification is performed by this document.

### Exactness and unresolved mechanics

**WR-023 remains a confirmed synthetic defect:** the accepted requirement is globally maximal totals with exact stored-float ordering, no tolerance. At Decimal precision 28, player 1 (slot 1 only, 1e30) plus players 2/3 (slot 2 only, 1.0/2.0) can select (1,2), although exact Fraction arithmetic favors (1,3). Decimal.from_float conversion does not make ambient-context addition exact. Allocation/reference helpers share this risk. [Mechanics obligations](BACKLOG.md#mechanics-review-obligations) require separately approved arithmetic correction, independent extreme/ordinary/tie evidence, valuation/transaction consequences, preserved unaffected identities and explicit changed-output review. This chapter changes no engine, reference or golden.

**WR-024 remains a source-inspected discrepancy:** production `_enumerate_plans` excludes incomplete V(R) alternatives and `_choose` may select a complete alternative. The independent sequential reference instead reports `determination_complete=False` if unresolved choices could affect ranking; equivalence cannot certify them. The approved requirement prohibits claiming an exact maximum after silently omitting materially unresolved plans. A separately scoped mixed complete/incomplete eligible-plan fixture, precise diagnostics, no zero substitution and season incomplete-propagation review remain required. This is neither a new live incident nor a documentation repair of the defect.

### Precision claims and WR-025

The governing target remains **95% family confidence and at most 0.5 probability-point half-width** (`C=0.95`, `h=0.005`), subject to representative benchmarking/calibration and minimum/budget controls. All-team absolute baseline targets and paired championship-delta targets are distinct. Editable quick/detailed h=0.03/0.01 presets do not replace that requirement. Path/time caps must disclose exposure, unmet targets and incompleteness. No preset is a convergence guarantee.

Baseline planned looks allocate over `M=T*(T+2)` metrics and `L=1+ceil((n_max-n_min)/d)` looks; Wilson remains an approximation. Progressive candidate-versus-leader contrasts allocate over unordered selected-state pairs and planned looks. **WR-025:** repeated all-team paired precision/display calls use the one-comparison `comparison_count=T*(T+2)` and two-direction Wilson allocation, with **no planned-look/across-state factor**. The selection-contrast allocation cannot certify that repeated precision/stopping family. [The exact outstanding requirement](BACKLOG.md#numerical-and-partial-evidence-obligations) retains family definition, theoretical and seeded known-probability/optional-stopping evidence, equal initial exposure, exact prefixes, finalist safeguards and independent review. No empirical undercoverage incident or replacement method is asserted. Any approved change must version altered interval/stopping/identity compatibility rather than rewrite historical goldens.

## Empirical validity

The question is whether a method predicts or supports decisions on authentic, representative future cases. A synthetic oracle can prove that code implements an equation or preserves path identity; it cannot prove that an ESPN forecast existed before kickoff, that its error distribution is calibrated, or that a decision proxy improves championships. [M2](METHODOLOGY.md#player-outcome-generation-and-assumptions), [M6](METHODOLOGY.md#monte-carlo-simulation-and-estimators) and [M7](METHODOLOGY.md#statistical-inference-precision-and-uncertainty) describe the mathematical experiment. Monte Carlo sampling uncertainty conditional on a model is separate from empirical model/data uncertainty.

### Evidence admissibility before evaluation

Start with [D2 source trust](DATA.md#source-trust-coverage-and-research-data). A usable projection/error pair needs a raw forecast and an actual, matching player/season/week and scoring context. Missing and policy-imputed-zero projections are excluded; explicit ESPN zero and signed projections remain observations. An actual zero is an outcome, not proof of nonparticipation. Historical universe completeness and point-in-time player/scoring context remain uncertain even when a pair exists.

For authenticated pregame evidence, [RESEARCH’s gate](RESEARCH.md#external-trust-and-exact-row-binding) requires an independently provisioned trusted manifest digest, exact payload bytes and capture strictly before the observation period. The accepted parser reparses those bytes; dataset construction binds every row's player, season/week, projection, position and NFL team, plus identical source metadata and period boundary. An invented player or modified projection cannot inherit authentication from a legitimate source ID. Actuals require a separate source retrieved at or after period end. [T2](TECHNICAL_REFERENCE.md#research-source-and-dataset-interfaces) describes schemas and validation interfaces.

The validator tests structural authentication, not who really provisioned a hash. Hashing one's own untrusted manifest does not establish independent trust. Operational evidence needs an independently controlled registry/review record and retained private bytes. No authenticated live capture program is established by this pilot.

### Temporal evaluation and support

The implemented [research interface](RESEARCH.md#temporal-evaluation-and-candidates) validates dataset identity and uses one explicit chronological boundary `(evaluation_start_season, evaluation_start_week)`: earlier periods train, boundary-or-later periods evaluate. There is no random player-week split. **This is not a sealed final-test process or automated protection against repeated tuning.** The governing promotion program still requires temporal training/validation/sealed-test separation and tuning isolation, with prospective/held-out evidence where feasible.

`AUTHENTICATED_ONLY` includes exact-row-bound pregame pairs. `INCLUDE_UNVERIFIED_EXPLORATORY` permits exploratory comparisons but cannot produce a calibrated-model recommendation. Missing/insufficient materialized data yields an insufficiency report, not fabricated scores. Configured minimum counts are execution gates, not universal scientific sufficiency thresholds.

Nine default candidates compare empirical and zero-mass Normal residuals across pooled, position, projection-band and joint conditioning, plus joint empirical hybrid partial pooling. Actual zero mass is separate; nonzero residuals translate to the target projection without flooring signed support. Normal research forecasts use deterministic quantile quadrature, not a change to production draws. Empirical/Normal sparse groups use pooled-only fallback. Hybrid empty groups use pooled-only; nonempty sparse groups retain shrunk local evidence and increment `sparse_partially_pooled_rows`, not `fallback_rows`. Audit coverage bands are not automatically model strata, and research pooling is not a missing-forecast production substitute. Mathematical outcome treatment remains in M2 and its current owner.

Apparent source metadata and additional Monte Carlo paths cannot repair these gaps.
