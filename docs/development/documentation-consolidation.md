## Decision and measurable target

Keep functional references, integrate new behavior into them, and use Git/PR history for ordinary delivery chronology. Do not archive reports merely because they were accepted. Do not delete a report until its unique current obligations, useful rationale and necessary evidence have reviewed homes.

The reviewed baseline has **74 Markdown files**, including three root documents. The proposal retains 39 of those document roles (one receives a functional filename), removes 35 baseline files after the stated transfers, and adds one specialized validation-evidence reference: **40 Markdown files**, a potential reduction of **34 / 45.9%** from 74. This plan temporarily makes 75; retiring it is included in the final package. Eight functional packages replace the 23 archive packages. Counts are a conditional planning result, not a deletion quota or a new test invariant.

The M0 manifest names seven documents: the retained dictionary plus five source reports and the missing-projection policy proposed for deletion. Two further removals need governing/release review. Holding those eight deletion candidates would leave **48**, rather than 40, after the other work and retirement of this plan. No deadline or preservation stub is invented to force the target. Supporting assets are counted separately; the 40-file target does not assume any PNG, JSON, tool or test deletion.

The largest maintenance improvements are removing two copies of the delivery narrative (MASTER_SPEC §12 and ROADMAP), deleting R1/R23/R25/R26 planning reports after their obligations are accounted for, and replacing six UI delivery documents with current user/design references. Remove duplicated setup recipes and API route lists at the same time. Measure success by one owner per explanation, finding the right current contract, preserved technical detail and fewer competing instructions, as well as file count. No net word-count saving is promised before content transfer; the deletion candidates contain substantive material.
