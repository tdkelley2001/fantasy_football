## Authority and target

Maintain three forms of information:

- **Current domain documents:** the single authoritative specification and explanation of accepted implemented behavior, algorithms, assumptions, interfaces, limitations and necessary rationale.
- **BACKLOG:** proposals, approved unimplemented requirements, unresolved decisions/evidence, priorities and delivery status.
- **Git/PRs:** completed milestones, previous implementations, ordinary verification and development chronology.

There will be **no separate MASTER_SPEC**, replacement master under another name, permanent migration catalog or general validation-history document. A small architecture map describes dependencies; it cannot restate or override domain rules. README owns the product/capability summary and navigation, replacing the separate status/index layer.

The design yields **17 Markdown documents**, counting the three root files. This is a consequence of the responsibilities below, within the requested approximate 12–18 range, not a quota. Of the 74 accepted-baseline files, 11 existing paths remain and six replacement references are created; 63 old paths become deletion candidates only after incorporation/review: 74 − 63 + 6 = 17, a potential reduction of 57 (77%). This plan temporarily makes 75; its retirement is additional. Necessary retained consumers or unresolved requirements can delay reductions. No count is an acceptance substitute.

Compared with the original proposal: merge the data quartet and rule/live-source references; combine season/postseason; combine evolving transactions, essential acquisitions, valuation and their reference explanation; combine the five action-search references; combine API/lifecycle; combine UI/task/design references; combine installation/operations and developer guidance. Preserve separate numerical, partial-evidence, research and counterfactual contracts for the specific reasons below. Do not create VALIDATION_EVIDENCE: retain selected evidence in its owning domain and executable artifacts, not another maintained report.
