# Weekly Essential Transactions (M2.3d)

Status: **COMPLETE AND ACCEPTED**. Governing requirements: `MASTER_SPEC.md` Sections 5 and 7 and the M2.3d gate.

## Contract

`execute_weekly_essential_transactions(snapshot, state, policy)` is a pure, deterministic one-window executor. It validates canonical schema v3 and the M2.3b evolving state, then returns `WeeklyTransactionResult` containing a new state, phase/pass counts, initial/frozen/final priorities, selected acquisitions, compact team decisions, rejection counts, assumptions, candidate-player count, correction states explored, attempted sequences, unique legal effects, V(R) evaluation count, and measured valuation time. Inputs are never modified and no ESPN request occurs.

`WeeklyTransactionPolicy.waiver_boundary` accepts only the typed `WaiverBoundaryPolicy` values `UNRESOLVED` and `MODELED_WEEKLY_V1`. The latter is the approved simulation boundary and is labeled `modeled_weekly_waiver_boundary_v1`; an arbitrary string cannot authorize processing. No live ESPN boundary provider is implemented. This boundary authorizes the modeled phase only: it neither clears a particular player nor releases a hold. There is no fallback for unknown ESPN clearance time. Materially incomplete M2.3c results cannot be compared as exact values.

## Qualification, ranking, and execution

Qualification uses M2.1 on owned players only. A team is eligible only with at least one mandatory positive-projection starter vacancy, and a plan qualifies only if its resulting owned roster strictly reduces that count. A stronger replacement for an already fillable slot is not essential.

For every phase-eligible unowned player, the engine enumerates configured legal destinations and every possible necessary owned-player drop. It performs a finite state search over M2.3b-authorized reactive IR corrections. Projection-mode recovered occupants and strict-mode occupants with validated current-week `INELIGIBLE` evidence are activation requirements; strict `ELIGIBLE` occupants remain legal, while missing/`UNKNOWN` evidence is unresolved. Every verified `OUT`/`IR` ordinary occupant is eligible for graph exploration, even when its slot cannot hold the acquisition candidate, because that placement may enable a required activation.

The search can combine multiple placements, required activations, activation-capacity drops, and the final add/drop in every legal order. Each player can consume ordinary-to-IR or required IR-to-ordinary movement at most once, and drops are irreversible in the cycle, which proves finite termination without a depth cap. Search identity retains roster, ownership, availability, counts, evidence and transition-history flags that affect future legality. Effect-equivalent final states may be canonicalized deterministically; materially different availability or ledger effects are retained.

Relevance is decided only on a complete acquisition path. Every IR placement must release an ordinary slot-capacity unit consumed later by a required activation or the final add. Each released unit has a distinct later consumer. Activation drops and acquisition drops are explored only when the corresponding no-drop operation is illegal. Thus an intermediate move need not improve coverage locally, but unrelated IR movement and unnecessary drops cannot enter V(R) ranking. The final owned lineup alone must strictly reduce baseline vacancies.

The credential-free test oracle independently enumerates all legal bounded correction sequences without calling the production graph, using only M2.3b primitives and the same finite player-movement constraints. It performs no production deduplication or forward relevance pruning, attempts the final acquisition at every leaf, and then compares complete legal effect sets, essential-qualified effects, ownership/occupancy/hold state and V(R). In the decisive regression, a zero-projection injured QB moves to IR, a recovered protected QB activates into the freed QB slot, and a WR/FLEX player is acquired even though the acquisition is not eligible for QB. Production and oracle result maps and maximum V(R) agree exactly; the oracle includes genuine two-step correction paths.

Diagnostics preserve three stages. `legally_executable_plans` counts attempted sequences whose primitives all succeed; `essential_qualified_plans` is the subset strictly reducing owned-lineup vacancies; `valuation_complete_plans` is the subset with comparable complete V(R). Counts are sequence counts. `unique_legal_resulting_states` separately counts deduplicated legal effects. Rejections distinguish unresolved authorization, protection, limits, capacity, availability, non-improving coverage, incomplete valuation, and structural ineligibility. Every qualifying result is valued with the same equal-weight remaining-season V(R). Maximum resulting V(R) wins; exact ties use acquired player, dropped players, IR movements, and destination slot IDs. No top-N or preferred-position pruning is used.

## Phase ordering

Waiver processing visits the dynamic priority order once per pass. Each team can succeed once in a pass, each successful claimant moves to last, and needs/options are recomputed after every mutation. Passes continue until a complete pass makes no acquisition.

Immediate free agents are then visited in reverse of the priority frozen at the start of the window. This approved V1 behavioral assumption is not an ESPN rule. The order remains fixed even if waiver claims changed dynamic priority, free-agent additions do not change priority, and repeated passes permit multiple essential fills.

The shared state updates ownership immediately. Dropped players remain held and prohibited from the same cycle; no release occurs without M2.3b validated authorization. Acquisition counters, position/roster capacities, undroppable protection, IR evidence, and transaction ledger remain authoritative in M2.3b.

## Termination and limitations

Every success strictly reduces the acting team's current-week vacancy count, removes an acquired player from the pool, and prevents dropped-player same-cycle return. A phase ends on a no-progress pass; there is no arbitrary iteration cap.

This is the approved sequential greedy manager policy, not a globally coordinated policy and not reconstruction of real submitted ESPN claims. M2.3e now validates it against an independently implemented sequential reference and separately enumerates every legally feasible outcome in declared small universes, including explicit pass branches. Legal enumeration is independent of V(R) completeness. If material valuation uncertainty makes the best sequential action unrankable, the reference fails closed and does not certify equivalence. Counterfactual path knowledge never changes the operational decision. See `TRANSACTION_CYCLE_REFERENCE.md`. Exact ESPN waiver timing and future strict-mode IR evidence remain externally unresolved. The engine records those restrictions rather than authorizing them. Detailed rejected candidates are summarized by reason to keep diagnostics bounded.

## Test and performance evidence

All **363 credential-free repository tests pass**. Sanitized focused tests cover no-op staffed teams, genuine and ineligible vacancies, FLEX/overlapping-slot repeated passes, contested shared free agents, explicit add/drop holds, necessary and compound IR placement, placement→activation→acquisition outside the acquisition slot namespace, independent oracle equivalence, strict `ELIGIBLE`/`INELIGIBLE`/`UNKNOWN` evidence, finite acquisition limits, controlled waiver authorization, stage-count reconciliation, waiver priority movement, unresolved waiver boundaries, deterministic reordered inputs, no network, immutability, and M2.1/M2.3c compatibility.

On 20 October 2026, the final corrected synthetic 14-team, three-week fixture with 24 pool players, four simultaneous essential shortages, and overlapping starter eligibility counted 106 candidate-player visits and correction states, attempted 923 sequences, produced 291 unique legal effects, and performed 68 complete V(R) evaluations. Four acquisitions completed in two free-agent passes in **16.155 seconds**, of which **12.266 seconds** was inside V(R). The reduction from the prior run comes from excluding demonstrably unnecessary drops, not candidate pruning. A separate four-player `tracemalloc` run attempted 223 sequences, produced 71 unique legal effects and made 18 valuations in **21.249 seconds**, with **11.429 seconds** in V(R) and **0.677 MiB** peak traced Python memory. The compound production/oracle regression completed in **0.15 seconds** under pytest. These are synthetic measurements, not live ESPN evidence. Exact repeated V(R) evaluation remains expensive and combinatorial; no candidate coverage was silently discarded to meet a runtime target.
